Climate & Precipitation

High-resolution atmospheric and hydrological layers providing temperature, precipitation, and moisture covariates.

Precipitation / Daily

CHIRPS Daily Precipitation

Bands: precipitation (Rainfall mm/day)
Resolution: 5,566 meters (~0.05°)
Source: UCSB / Climate Hazards Center

Climate Hazards Group InfraRed Precipitation with Station data (CHIRPS) is a 30+ year quasi-global rainfall dataset. It merges satellite imagery with in-situ station data to create gridded precipitation time series ideal for trend analysis.

Atmosphere / Daily

ERA5 Daily Aggregates

Bands: mean_2m_air_temperature, total_precipitation, dewpoint_2m_temperature
Resolution: 27,830 meters (~0.25°)
Source: ECMWF / Copernicus

ERA5 is the fifth generation ECMWF atmospheric reanalysis of the global climate. It combines model data with observations from across the world to provide daily temperature, humidity, and atmospheric metrics.

View in GEE Catalog ↗
Land Climate / Monthly

ERA5-Land Monthly Aggregates

Bands: temperature_2m, total_precipitation_sum, dewpoint_temperature_2m
Resolution: 11,132 meters (~0.1°)
Source: ECMWF / Copernicus

A high-resolution dataset focusing on the land surface. It provides enhanced monthly temperature and water cycle parameters, offering greater spatial detail than standard ERA5 for land-based ecological studies.

Vegetation & Land Surface

Satellite observations tracking canopy greenness, land surface temperature anomalies, and thermal signatures.

Vegetation / 16-Day

MODIS NDVI (MOD13Q1)

Bands: NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)
Resolution: 250 meters
Source: NASA / USGS LP DAAC

Provides consistent spatial and temporal comparisons of global vegetation conditions. Used for monitoring photosynthetic activity, canopy density, forest health, and land cover dynamics over time.

View in GEE Catalog ↗
Land Temperature / Monthly

MODIS Land Surface Temp (LST)

Bands: LST_Day_1km, LST_Night_1km (aggregated from MOD21C3)
Resolution: 5,600 meters (~0.05°)
Source: NASA / USGS LP DAAC

Monthly land surface temperature (LST) values, split into Daytime and Nighttime bands. Excellent for calculating diurnal temperature ranges, thermal stress, and micro-climate patterns.

View in GEE Catalog ↗

Elevation & Topography

High-resolution digital elevation models capturing physical geography, terrain slopes, and mountainous barriers.

Elevation / Static

SRTM Elevation (30m)

Bands: elevation (Meters above sea level)
Resolution: 30 meters
Source: NASA / USGS

The Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM) provides near-global digital elevation data. Critical for environmental studies mapping altitude barriers, river watersheds, terrain aspect, and slope gradients.
